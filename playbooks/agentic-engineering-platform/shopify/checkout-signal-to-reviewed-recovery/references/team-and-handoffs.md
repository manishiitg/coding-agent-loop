# Checkout recovery handoff

This route reuses Shopify Growth Analyst for a bounded signal, then Checkout Recovery Coordinator for contact eligibility. It adds route-specific instructions to the existing Growth Crew; the generic storefront-growth skill does not by itself authorize reading customer records or contacting shoppers. The handoff contains IDs and a hypothesis, not an address, email, phone, or recovery URL.

## `checkout-recovery-signal/v1`

Required fields: `artifact_type`, `store_id`, `market`, `checkout_id`, `campaign_id`, `channel` (`email` or `sms`), `observed_at`, `signal_kind` (`abandoned_checkout`), `hypothesis`, `owner_id`, and nonempty `source_refs`. A query hit alone is not an eligibility decision: Shopify's [AbandonedCheckout query](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/queries/abandonedCheckouts) can include recovered checkouts.

## `checkout-recovery-review/v1`

Match store, market, checkout, campaign, and channel exactly. Include `artifact_type`, `observed_at`, `policy_version`, `checkout_completed_at` (nullable), `later_order_state` (`none_verified`, `linked_order_observed`, `unknown`), `consent_state` (`subscribed`, `not_subscribed`, `unknown`), `contact_policy_state` (`allowed`, `blocked`, `unknown`), `prior_send_state` (`none_verified`, `sent_or_scheduled`, `unknown`), `suppression_state` (`clear`, `blocked`, `unknown`), `decision` (`suppress`, `needs_information`, `draft_review`), `draft_text` (nullable), `approval_state` (`pending`, `approved`, `rejected`), `send_state` (`not_sent`, `sent`), `recovery_state` (`not_observed`, `linked_order_observed`), `owner_id`, `next_evidence`, and nonempty `source_refs`. Optional refs are `approval_ref`, `pre_send_recheck_ref`, `provider_send_ref`, `linked_order_ref`, and `checkout_order_link_ref`; `send_observed_at` and `recovery_observed_at` become required when those later states are claimed.

The completion, later-order, consent, and suppression fields describe the eligibility snapshot at `observed_at`. `draft_review` needs an incomplete checkout, verified absence of a later linked order and prior send, subscribed channel consent, allowed merchant policy, clear suppression, and a draft. It also needs six distinct proof fields: `checkout_state_ref`, `order_lookup_ref`, `consent_ref`, `policy_ref`, `suppression_ref`, and `message_history_ref`. A source reference is a review pointer, not proof that the source is true; the merchant verifies it. `suppress` needs an explicit blocker. Unknown evidence must route to `needs_information` or suppression. `sent` is a later state that requires approved owner decision, a provider receipt, observation time, and a fresh pre-send source check recorded in the action ledger. `recovery_state: linked_order_observed` is another later state; it needs exact order, trusted checkout/order link, and observation time. It does not prove the message caused the order. Never infer a link by matching email or amount.

## Source, privacy and repeat policy

The [GraphQL AbandonedCheckout](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/AbandonedCheckout) requires authorized order access and permission; customer consent may need further customer access. Shopify's [Messaging guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/migrate-abandoned-checkout) notes that a store may already have an abandoned checkout automation, so inspect it before proposing another. Apply the merchant's actual channel, market and legal policy; the validator checks declared states, not legal validity. Do not place contact data or recovery links in shared artifacts.

For recurrence, use the stable store-checkout-channel-campaign key. Verify HTTPS webhook signatures where applicable, deduplicate deliveries, re-read the live checkout/order/consent/provider records, and stop after recovery, opt-out, suppression, or an existing send. [Shopify webhook guidance](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/webhooks/verify-deliveries) describes signature and duplicate-delivery handling. A scheduled run is read-only until the merchant explicitly approves a separate send route.
