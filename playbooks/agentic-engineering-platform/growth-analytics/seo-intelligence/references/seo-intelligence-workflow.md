# SEO intelligence workflow

## Define the market before analysis

Version the keyword universe (topics, intent, locales, devices), competitor set, technical standards (crawlability, indexation, metadata, internal linking, page experience), ranking and traffic targets, and attribution window. Reuse customer definitions when their scope and windows are compatible; never silently narrow the universe to improve a trend.

Record rank-sample rules: tracked keywords, locales, devices, and sampling limits. A ranking claim is only valid for its sampled universe, never as a whole-market census.

## Adapt the plan

Begin with the two-Crew manual route described in [team and handoff](team-and-handoffs.md). SEO Analyst captures a bounded technical issue list; Search Opportunity Mapper relates its exact artifact to buyer questions and inspected pages. Put a blocking validator after each saved Crew artifact. Do not require a keyword universe or rank tracker for a newly launched site: an owner-sourced question and public page capture can produce a useful first review while demand remains unknown.

When Search Console is authorized, use scripted pulls to compare the same property, query/page, locale, device, and equal windows. Validate clicks, impressions, CTR arithmetic, data lag and coverage. Use a message sequence to separate demand shifts from ranking shifts and technical breakage, test alternative explanations (tracking change, seasonality, site release, migration), and draft page briefs with evidence. Briefs propose changes; they do not apply them.

For recurring monitoring, prove an on-demand two-Crew analysis first, then propose a paused schedule or threshold alert with explicit scope, cadence, timezone, crawl budget, source freshness and notification conditions.

## Validation and report

Validate SEO reproducibility from durable snapshots, denominator presence on every rate, locale/window compatibility, Search Console reconciliation, crawl coverage of the authorized scope, brief-evidence linkage, and evidence for every finding. Verify that site releases and crawler blocks surface as data-quality events rather than silent traffic shifts.

Build a report showing technical health by exact issue and page, buyer-question opportunities, confidence, limitations, owner decisions and history. Show rankings or trend lines only when their authorized source and comparable windows pass the policy. Incomplete or untrusted states stay visibly unrated.

## Handoff

Website Growth Loop may consume a separately reviewed opportunity when the owner elects content production and publication. Pass frozen artifact IDs, source links, blocker status and approval state; do not require downstream Crews to reconstruct a finding from raw pulls or chat history. A draft or approval is not a verified publication or traffic result.
