# SEO intelligence workflow

## Define the market before analysis

Version the keyword universe (topics, intent, locales, devices), competitor set, technical standards (crawlability, indexation, metadata, internal linking, page experience), ranking and traffic targets, and attribution window. Reuse customer definitions when their scope and windows are compatible; never silently narrow the universe to improve a trend.

Record rank-sample rules: tracked keywords, locales, devices, and sampling limits. A ranking claim is only valid for its sampled universe, never as a whole-market census.

## Adapt the plan

Use scripted steps for rank snapshots, authorized crawls, Search Console pulls, gap scoring, internal-link mapping, and completeness checks (freshness, coverage, reconciliation, missing pages). A change is reportable only when it clears the customer's minimum detectable effect and data-quality gate.

Use a message sequence to investigate a supported change: separate demand shifts from ranking shifts from technical breakage, test alternative explanations (algorithm update, tracking change, seasonality, site release, migration), rank opportunities by winnability and value, and draft page briefs with evidence. Briefs propose changes; they do not apply them.

For recurring monitoring, prove an on-demand analysis first, then configure scheduled SEO runs or threshold alerts with explicit scope, cadence, timezone, and notification conditions.

## Validation and report

Validate SEO reproducibility from durable snapshots, denominator presence on every rate, locale/window compatibility, Search Console reconciliation, crawl coverage of the authorized scope, brief-evidence linkage, and evidence for every finding. Verify that site releases and crawler blocks surface as data-quality events rather than silent traffic shifts.

Build a live SEO dashboard showing rankings with trends, technical health by issue and page, content and linking gaps with scores, prioritized opportunities, page briefs, confidence, limitations, and history. Incomplete or untrusted states stay visibly unrated.

## Handoff

Growth Experimentation and Follow-Through consumes frozen findings with their evidence and confidence. Return SEO/policy versions, usable windows, ranked opportunities, page briefs, and recommended hypotheses. Do not require downstream agents to recompute rankings or crawls from raw pulls or chat history.
