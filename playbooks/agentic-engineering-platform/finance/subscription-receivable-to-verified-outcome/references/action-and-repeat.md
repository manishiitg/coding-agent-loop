# Reviewed action and repeat cycle

Start with current invoice status and due balance. Distinguish **overdue but no failed attempt**, **failed attempt with a provider retry pending**, **failed with no retry**, **disputed/suppressed**, and **paid after the cutoff**. Use the customer's policy to choose wait, review, or draft a reminder. A queued provider retry should normally prevent an overlapping manual charge attempt; record the actual policy decision. An unverified contact history blocks a send claim and should trigger a current-source check.

A draft is reviewable output. Approval identifies the exact customer, invoice, message, channel and action key. Delivery needs a provider receipt for the same action key and a last-minute suppression and duplicate check. The Playbook never grants send authority by being selected. A failed delivery remains failed even if the message was approved.

For a repeat run, load prior case and action IDs, provider event IDs, last contact, last payment state and owner decision. Re-read the invoice before proposing another reminder or interpreting a retry event. Preserve a stable case ID; record changed evidence and new status rather than creating a fresh-looking case. Report no new evidence and a next check if neither balance nor policy state changed.

Finance observes newly collected amount in minor units with exact payment receipt. Reconcile the post-cutoff remaining amount against the Billing cutoff. A provider collection can be partial or complete while its payout is pending. For a verified deposit, match the invoice payment to a provider balance transaction, compute its net after fees, account for the other net transactions in the full payout, and match the full payout amount to separate bank and ledger sources. Keep an unallocated or complex payout unsettled until the owner can reconcile it. See [Stripe's balance transaction amount, fee and net fields](https://docs.stripe.com/api/balance_transactions/object) and [payout amount and status](https://docs.stripe.com/api/payouts/object) as provider-specific examples. If payment, credit or invoice state changed outside this case, stop and reconcile the new source revision before using the old arithmetic.
