# AI Visibility Intelligence

## Outcome

Create versioned prompt-based citation tracking that shows when AI assistants mention or cite the brand, how competitors compare, and which content and authority changes would close the gap.

## When to use

Use after Growth Data Foundation covers traffic sources, or an equivalent prompt-tracking setup exists. Use it for AI-search presence growth, launch readouts, citation-drop investigation, and recurring visibility monitoring. It does not own keyword rankings, crawls, or technical SEO; those belong to SEO Intelligence.

## Discovery and user direction

Inspect the current workflow, goals, metrics, prompt set, source coverage, capabilities, stores, dashboards, and schedules before proposing changes. Summarize reusable foundations and gaps, ask focused questions for unresolved scope, assistants, competitors, sampling, ownership, and success criteria, and record the answers as customer direction. Installation alone does not approve workflow changes or execution.

## Required inputs

Resolve prompt set with personas and locales, assistant scope and sampling policy, competitor brand set, citation and sentiment definitions, targets or thresholds, minimum data quality, reporting audience, and decision ownership.

## Plan and AgentWorks tools

Use scripted steps for deterministic prompt runs, citation extraction, competitor comparison, sentiment scoring, and completeness checks. Use a message sequence to interpret supported shifts, diagnose likely causes, prioritize gaps, and recommend content and authority changes. Prove an on-demand analysis first, then recommend scheduled monitoring with explicit scope, cadence, timezone, and notification policy. Keep publication behind configured review.

## Knowledge and persistence

Store prompt sets, citation snapshots, competitor comparisons, gap analyses, recommendations, and follow-up outcomes in durable tables. Keep customer definitions/preferences in KB context and verified interpretation rules in notes or learnings. Preserve answer excerpts within fair-use limits with source references rather than full reproductions.

## Validation and reporting

Validate prompt-set versioning, assistant coverage, sample integrity, citation-attribution rules, comparison compatibility, recommendation-evidence linkage, and evidence for every finding. The dashboard exposes citation share, trends, competitor comparison, gaps, recommendations, confidence, limitations, and drill-downs to prompts and answers.

## Guardrails

Do not present a prompt sample as an exhaustive census, compare different prompts/locales/assistants as equals, fabricate reviews or testimonials, plant deceptive content, or recommend manipulation of AI answers. Disclose personalization and sampling limits on every claim.
