# Engineering and Shopify template expansion

Date: 2026-09-26. This is an authoring queue, not a list of installed templates. The library currently has **13 Engineering Crew capabilities** when QA (3) and Security (3) are browsed under Engineering's 7 core roles, and **7 Shopify Crews**. The existing 71 Crew IDs and 55 Playbook packages do not change with browse grouping.

## Selection rule

Add a Crew only for a recurring human job with a distinct owner, source access, decision, and first reviewable artifact. If an installed Crew can perform the job with a scoped skill, add a capability pack instead. A new multi-Crew Playbook needs an exact handoff, an owner-reviewed action boundary, real-source setup checks, valid and rejected artifacts, and a manual first case. Package selection never activates a write or schedule.

## Engineering: next distinct jobs

| Priority | Candidate Crew and job | First artifact and source proof | Boundary and Playbook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Customer Defect Investigator.** Take one Support-reported product defect from exact case/build to reproducible or explicitly unknown Engineering evidence. Browser Journey QA tests an approved journey; Delivery Coordinator tracks an accepted change. Neither owns the case-to-defect diagnosis. | Case and customer scope, affected build/environment, current logs or trace, bounded reproduction, existing issue match, Engineering owner and next evidence. Reject a support severity label as reproduction or an issue title as owner acceptance. | Proposed **Support Escalation to Verified Fix**: accepted Support case → defect finding → owned change/deployment → independent QA retest → unsent Support update. Never mark merged code as deployed or a draft as delivered. |
| 2 | **Service Reliability & Capacity Analyst.** Review a service's SLO, demand, saturation and capacity headroom over comparable windows for an accountable service owner. Incident Investigator handles acute failures; Performance Investigator handles a bounded performance question. | Versioned SLI/SLO and service identity, error-budget arithmetic, traffic/capacity sources and coverage, forecast assumptions, owner decision. Insufficient instrumentation returns unknown rather than a safe-to-grow claim. | Proposed **SLO Risk to Owned Capacity Action**: observation → reviewed capacity/change plan → later deployed and measured state. No automatic infrastructure scale or purchasing action. |

**Capability packs before new identities:** dependency upgrades should first extend Engineering Delivery Coordinator with exact package/advisory, compatibility, CI, deployed version and rollback checks. A data-pipeline integrity role should wait for a merchant or SaaS pilot that proves a separate data owner and source-to-destination reconciliation job. GitHub's [Dependabot version update guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/concepts/supply-chain-security/dependabot-version-updates) illustrates why a proposed update is only an input to release review; Google's [SRE operational efficiency guidance](https://sre.google/workbook/eliminating-toil/) motivates service objectives, measurement and capacity work.

## Shopify: next distinct merchant jobs

| Priority | Candidate Crew and job | First artifact and source proof | Boundary and Playbook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Order Risk Review Coordinator.** Decide whether one flagged order should be held for merchant review, investigated or safely released to fulfillment. Payment Operations Investigator checks transaction state, not order-risk evidence. | Exact store/order and risk-assessment revisions, payment state, fulfillment state, merchant risk policy, owner and next check. A risk score alone is not fraud proof. | Proposed **Order Risk to Fulfillment Decision** with Store Operations Coordinator. No cancellation, capture, hold release or customer message from installing or producing a review. Shopify exposes [OrderRiskAssessment](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/OrderRiskAssessment). |
| 2 | **B2B Wholesale Coordinator.** Reconcile one company location's approved catalog, price, tax and payment terms before a reviewed quote or order. This differs from consumer catalog cleanup. | Exact company/location/contact, catalog and price-list revisions, draft/order ID, payment terms, owner decision and mismatches. Reject a default market price as the customer's negotiated price. | Proposed **B2B Terms to Reviewed Order** with Catalog & Merchandising and Store Operations, reusing an existing Crew when the same owner has access. No quote or order submission without merchant review. Shopify's [B2B model](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/b2b) joins companies, locations, contacts, catalogs and terms. |
| 3 | **Promotion & Discount Auditor.** Check one promotion's eligibility, stacking, market price and actual checkout result against merchant margin policy. Growth Analyst measures an outcome; Catalog Analyst fixes product facts. | Discount/version, market/catalog/price, test cart and checkout observation, margin inputs, dates and owner. Reject a configured discount as proof it applied correctly. | Proposed **Promotion to Verified Checkout** with Shopify Growth Analyst for a later comparable result. Creating or changing a discount is a separate approved action. Shopify documents [discount classes and combinations](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/discounts). |
| 4 | **Subscription Commerce Coordinator**, only for merchants actually using a Shopify subscription app. Reconcile contract, billing attempt, generated order and fulfillment or customer exception for one cycle. | Exact app-owned contract and cycle, attempt state, order ID, payment/fulfillment records, retry policy and owner. A pending billing attempt is not an order or successful renewal. | Proposed **Subscription Failure to Reconciled Order** with Payment Operations Investigator and Store Operations. No automatic charge retry or contract edit. Shopify's [contract and billing-attempt model](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/purchase-options/subscriptions/contracts/build-a-subscription-contract) makes the application owner and idempotency key essential. |

Shopify customer-service triage and retention remain shared Customer Support or Marketing/Customer Success capabilities with Shopify-specific source joins, as described in the [operator category guide](shopify_operator_category.md). They should not become duplicate Crew IDs solely to increase the count.

## Release order

First verify the Engineering browse grouping and package installation on one Crew and one Playbook. Next author the customer-defect and order-risk routes because their owner and evidence gaps are clearest. Validate B2B, promotions and subscriptions with a merchant before promoting them from candidates. Each new package must pass the Playbook validator, frontend catalog projection and one customer-like manual setup case before it is called operationally ready.
