# Observability and managed-service cost analysis

## Observability

Break down log, metric, and trace cost by ingestion, indexed volume, retention/tier, cardinality, query/scan, archive, export, and transfer. Collect source/team, signal purpose, volume/cardinality trend, query/dashboard/alert use, retention and audit rules, sampling, duplication, and incident value.

Candidates can remove duplicate telemetry, filter low-value fields/events at the source, control cardinality, adjust trace sampling, or tier retention. Preserve security/audit evidence, incident diagnostics, SLO signals, and required retention. Verify alerting, investigations, dashboards, query latency, coverage, and net cost.

## Cache, queue, and search

Reconcile nodes/shards, replicas, storage, provisioned throughput, transfer, backups, and requests. Collect utilization, working set, hit/eviction rate, queue lag, shard balance, latency, errors, failover, rebalance, retention, recovery, and peak demand. Model sizing, shard/replica, retention, or storage-tier changes only with recovery and failure behavior included.

## SaaS and licenses

Collect contract term, tier, seats/units, active use, owner, renewal, minimum commitment, overage, and cancellation terms. Flag dormant seats, duplicate products, and unused add-ons for owner review. Never remove access automatically. Count savings only after the contract or invoice reflects the approved change; separate negotiated savings from workload efficiency.
