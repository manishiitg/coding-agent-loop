# Tax Export Preparer setup

Template: `tax-export` version 1. Progress is saved in `templates/tax-export/TEMPLATE_SETUP.json`.

Start with uploaded records when available. A connected accounting MCP is optional; select and test it for this Crew only after the owner grants access. The owner may choose to keep the export in Crew chat or approve a secure delivery route. Do not copy another Crew's connection, secret, schedule, or trigger.

The first useful result is a sample export for an agreed period, reconciled to source totals, with an exceptions list. Ask the owner and their tax professional to confirm the format and classification rules. A recurring schedule or new-record trigger is optional and requires a separate setup decision and test.

The skill includes a fictional CSV sample and reconciliation showing a payment, refund, fee and bank transfer. Use it to review field mapping, signed amounts and duplicate handling, then test against the customer's own records. The bank transfer is not another customer receipt. Missing tax classifications remain pending for the professional; the example does not complete setup or establish tax treatment.
